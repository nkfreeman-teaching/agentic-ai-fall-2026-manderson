# Data sources

This session uses enforcement records from the U.S. Department of Labor's Wage and Hour Division (WHD), which publishes records of compliance actions concluded since fiscal year 2005.

- The [WHD enforcement dataset page](https://dataportal.dol.gov/datasets/10362) describes the data and lists its fields.
- The [direct ZIP download](https://data.dol.gov/data-catalog/WHD/enforcement/WHD_enforcement.zip) contains the data as CSV files.
- The [data dictionary](https://apiprod.dol.gov/v4/datasets/10362) is the department's JSON metadata, and its `dataset.dataset_metadatum` array gives each field's name, description, and data type.

A related source describes the temporary agricultural (H-2A) and non-agricultural (H-2B) visa programs:

- The [H-2A and H-2B disclosure files](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance) from the Office of Foreign Labor Certification describe employer applications to those programs.

The downloaded data are not tracked in this repository (see `.gitignore`).
